forge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24

forge is a private, internal tool used by its owner, an independent creator, to manage only his own YouTube channels (@fatiopassoutv, @respawnadogames, @cifracurta). This policy explains what data forge accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

forge uses YouTube API Services. By using forge you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, forge accesses:

  • Basic information about the owner's own channels (channel ID, title, description).
  • Metadata of the owner's own videos (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy and scheduling status).
  • Analytics reports of the owner's own channels and videos (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

forge does not access data of other users or other channels.

How the data is used

The data is used only to manage the owner's own channels: uploading and scheduling videos, editing their metadata and thumbnails, and reviewing their performance.

Storage, sharing and retention

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any third-party server.
  • Data is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally in files with restricted permissions (readable only by the owner's user account) and are deleted when access is revoked.
  • Analytics responses are cached locally for a few hours and refreshed from the API; cached data is not kept beyond what is needed to operate the tool.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, trackers, analytics or third-party scripts. forge itself does not place cookies on any user's device.

Revoking access

Access granted to forge can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, forge can no longer access the channel, and the locally stored tokens are deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
