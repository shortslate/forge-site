forge

forge is a private, internal tool used by its owner, an independent creator, to manage only his own YouTube channels: @fatiopassoutv, @respawnadogames and @cifracurta.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own Shorts and schedules their publication.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails of the owner's own videos.
  • Reads analytics of the owner's own channels to plan future content.

forge runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered, sold or made available to anyone else, and it has no other users.
